# NotesMigrator Support

Need help? Email **info.alayan@gmail.com** and include your device (Mac, iPhone, or iPad) and what you were trying to do. We usually reply within two business days.

## Frequently asked questions

### How do I get my Apple Notes into the app on iPhone or iPad?

iOS does not let other apps read Apple Notes directly. Use one of these:

- **Apple Shortcut:** in the app, tap **Install 'Export Apple Notes' Shortcut** and run it. It sends your notes to NotesMigrator.
- **Backup folder:** tap **Import Backup Folder** and choose an exported notes folder from iCloud Drive or Files.

### The Mac app can't read my notes

The first time you scan, macOS asks for permission to control Notes. If you declined, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation** and allow NotesMigrator to control **Notes**. To include scanned documents, follow the folder-access step in the app's Setup Assistant.

### How do I connect OneNote?

Open the Setup Assistant and sign in with your Microsoft account. Your notes go directly from your device to your own OneNote notebooks.

### What does the free version include?

You can migrate up to 5 notes for free so you can check the results in OneNote first.

### How does NotesMigrator Pro work?

NotesMigrator Pro is a yearly auto-renewable subscription that unlocks unlimited notes. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

- **Manage or cancel:** open your Apple ID account settings → **Subscriptions**.
- **Restore on a new device:** open the Pro screen in the app and tap **Restore Previous Purchase**.

### Is my data private?

Yes. We have no servers, analytics, or tracking. See the [Privacy Policy]({{ site.baseurl }}/).
