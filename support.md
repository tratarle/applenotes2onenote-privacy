# NotesMigrator Support

Need help? Email **info.alayan@gmail.com** and include your macOS version and what you were trying to do. We usually reply within two business days.

## Frequently asked questions

### The Mac app can't read my notes

The first time you scan, macOS asks for permission to control Notes. If you declined, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation** and allow NotesMigrator to control **Notes**. To include scanned documents, follow the folder-access step in the app's Setup Assistant.

### How do I connect OneNote?

Open the Setup Assistant and sign in with your Microsoft account. Your notes go directly from your device to your own OneNote notebooks.

### What does the free version include?

You can migrate up to 5 notes for free so you can check the results in OneNote first.

### How does NotesMigrator Pro work?

NotesMigrator Pro unlocks unlimited notes. Choose either a yearly auto-renewable subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period; the lifetime purchase never renews.

- **Manage or cancel the subscription:** open your Apple Account settings → **Subscriptions**.
- **Restore on a new device:** open the Pro screen in the app and tap **Restore Previous Purchase**.

### Is my data private?

Yes. We have no servers, analytics, or tracking. See the [Privacy Policy]({{ site.baseurl }}/).
